Crafting

How items get their mods, what each orb does, and the methods people use. Before spending real currency, try your craft for free on Craft of Exile: it shows every possible mod, its odds and the average cost.

How mods work

  • A rare item can have up to 3 prefixes and 3 suffixes. Life and flat damage are usually prefixes; resistances and attributes are usually suffixes.
  • Each mod has tiers. Higher tiers need a higher item level, so the base you craft on matters.
  • Each mod has a weight: how likely it is to roll. Rare, strong mods have low weights. That's why some crafts take hundreds of tries.
  • Influenced bases (Shaper, Elder, Conquerors) and Eldritch implicits unlock extra mods.
  • Mod pools differ per base type. PoEDB lists every mod and tier for each base, for example the crafting bench page.

What each orb does

Live prices next to each orb are from poe.ninja for your league.

Transmutation

Normal → magic (1–2 mods).

Alteration

Rerolls a magic item. The classic way to fish for one specific mod.

Augmentation

Adds a mod to a magic item with one mod.

Regal

Magic → rare, keeping the mods and adding one.

Alchemy

Normal → rare with random mods.

Chaos

Rerolls all mods on a rare. Also the main trade currency.

Exalted

Adds one random mod to a rare.

Annulment

Removes one random mod. Risky unless the other mods are protected.

Divine

Rerolls the numbers of mods within their tiers. The main high-value currency.

Scouring

Removes all mods, back to normal.

Blessed

Rerolls the numbers of implicit mods.

Vaal

Corrupts an item: unpredictable result, and it can't be changed afterwards.

Fusing · Jeweller's · Chromatic

Reroll links, socket count and socket colours.

Fracturing

Locks one random mod permanently. Fractured bases are the starting point for many high-end crafts.

Mirror of Kalandra

Duplicates an item. The rarest currency, used to copy perfect items.

The crafting bench

The bench in your hideout adds a chosen mod for a fixed cost, as long as the item has a free prefix or suffix.

  • Use it to fill the last open slot with resistance, life or damage. This is the cheapest upgrade in the game.
  • "Prefixes/Suffixes Cannot Be Changed" protects one side when you use chaos-style rerolls. "Can have up to 3 crafted modifiers" lets you add several bench mods (multimod).
  • You unlock recipes by finding them in the world; the list is on PoEDB.

Crafting methods

Alteration spam (cheap, for one or two mods)

Transmute a base, use Alterations until you hit the mod you want, Augment for a second one, then Regal into a rare and finish with the bench. Great for flasks, jewels and early-league gear.

Essence spam (guaranteed mod)

Each essence guarantees one mod and rolls the rest at random. Keep using essences until the random mods are good too. A good choice when the key mod is the one the essence gives.

Fossils and resonators (steer the mod pool)

Fossils add or block whole groups of mods, for example "more physical, no lightning". A resonator holds 1–4 fossils and rerolls the item like a Chaos Orb but with those rules. Good for items where most mods share a tag.

Metacrafting (protect what you have)

Bench-craft "Prefixes Cannot Be Changed", then reroll or annul the suffixes without touching the good prefixes. It costs more per try but is how most top-end items are finished.

Omens and special currency

Some league currencies change how a normal orb behaves (for example, making an Exalted Orb add only a prefix). They change from league to league; check the Market page for what's available.

Craft or buy?

  1. Open Build cost and paste your build. For the item you want, use Trade ↗ to see what it costs ready-made.
  2. On Craft of Exile, pick the same base and item level, choose your method and target mods, and read the average cost it calculates.
  3. If buying is cheaper, buy it. Crafting wins mostly for very specific mod combinations nobody sells.
Craft of Exile's emulator lets you click orbs on a virtual item for free. Practise a method there before trying it with real currency.
