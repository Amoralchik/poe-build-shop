Basics

The things every player needs to know, in plain words. Every topic links to PoEDB, the game database, for the exact numbers.

Pick a build

  • Start with a build guide instead of inventing one. Look at what's popular and working on poe.ninja builds; every character there has a Path of Building code.
  • Path of Building (PoB) is the free planner everyone uses. It shows your damage and defences and lets you test items before buying them.
  • Paste a build's PoB code into Build cost to see what it costs before you commit.
  • League starters should work with cheap gear. If a build needs expensive uniques to function, play it later.

Defences: don't skip these

  • Resistances. Fire, cold and lightning cap at 75%. Completing the campaign lowers them, so keep them capped on gear. Chaos resistance matters more later. PoEDB: resistance
  • Life or energy shield. Pick one main pool. Most builds want 4,000+ life for maps and much more for bosses.
  • Mitigation. Armour reduces hits, evasion avoids attacks, block stops hits, and spell suppression reduces spell damage. Good builds stack more than one layer.
  • Recovery. Life flasks, leech and regeneration keep you alive between hits.
  • Rule of thumb: if you die often, fix defences before adding damage.

Items and rarity

Normal

No mods. Used as crafting bases.

Magic

1 prefix and 1 suffix at most. Good for flasks and early levelling.

Rare

Up to 3 prefixes and 3 suffixes. Most of your gear will be rares.

Unique

Fixed special mods. Some builds are built around one unique.

  • Item level decides the best mod tiers an item can roll. Required level is what you need to wear it.
  • Sockets and links hold your skill gems. A skill gem plus linked support gems makes your main skill. A 6-link body armour or 2-handed weapon is the classic endgame upgrade.
  • Influence (Shaper, Elder, Conquerors) adds extra mod pools. PoEDB: influenced items
  • Corrupted items can't be changed any more, except by a few special currencies.

Currency

There's no gold-style money. Orbs are the money, and each also does something to items (see Crafting).

  • Chaos Orb is the everyday unit for prices.
  • Divine Orb is the big unit; expensive things are priced in divines.
  • Mirror of Kalandra is the rarest item in trade.
  • Check what anything is worth on the Market page.

Trading

  • Use the official trade site to search for items, then whisper the seller in game. BuildShop's Trade ↗ buttons open ready-made searches.
  • Swap currency without whispering using the in-game currency exchange.
  • Prices are in chaos or divine. Sort by price and skip listings that look far too cheap; they're often price-fixing bait.
  • Be polite, be quick, and say "ty" after a trade. It really does help.

The campaign

  • The story has 10 acts. Most of the game (maps, crafting, bosses) starts after Act 10.
  • Don't overgear while levelling. Keep resistances near the cap, use movement-speed boots, and keep flasks up to date.
  • Complete the Labyrinth trials to earn ascendancy points. There are four labs; do them as you level.
  • Pick a Pantheon power after the relevant bosses; it's free defence.

Maps and the atlas

  • After the campaign you run maps: items that open endgame areas. Higher tier maps are harder and richer.
  • Completing maps fills your atlas and gives atlas passive points. Spend them to boost the mechanics you want to farm (see Farming).
  • Beating the pinnacle bosses gives Voidstones, which raise all your maps to the highest tier. Getting all four is the usual endgame goal.
  • Your atlas can be adjusted at any time, so try a strategy and change it if the profit isn't there.
