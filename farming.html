Picking a strategy

League mechanics and atlas passives change every league. This page explains the core ideas that stay the same; check PoEDB for this league's exact passives and numbers.
  1. Fit your build. Fast clear builds suit mechanics with lots of monsters (Breach, Legion, Delirium). Tanky single-target builds suit bossing and hard content.
  2. Specialise. Put most atlas passives and map device modifiers into one or two mechanics. Doing a little of everything earns much less.
  3. Farm what sells in bulk. Currency-like loot (essences, oils, splinters) sells fast through bulk trade or the currency exchange. Rare gear is slow to sell.
  4. Count your costs. Scarabs, map modifiers and fragments are part of the price. Profit is what's left after them, which is exactly what Wealthy Exile's strategy pages track.
  5. Measure it. Run the same setup for an hour and compare your stash before and after. The Wealthy Exile stash tracker does this for you.

Essences

EasyLow investmentAny build

Essence monsters are frozen in crystals in maps. Opening one gives an essence; using a Remnant of Corruption before killing it can upgrade or change the essences, sometimes into the valuable special ones.

  • Essences sell in bulk to crafters. The top tier of each type and the special essences are worth the most.
  • Good first strategy of a league: safe, cheap, and the loot is easy to sell.

Breach

EasyFast clear

A Breach opens a growing circle full of monsters. Kill as many as you can before it closes. They drop splinters that combine into Breachstones, which open a boss domain.

  • Splinters and Breachstones sell in bulk. Some players buy Breachstones and sell the boss rewards instead.
  • Needs fast clear and good movement; very slow builds get little from it.
PoEDB: Breachpoe.ninja fragments

Legion

MediumFast clear + damage

Touching the Legion monolith frees frozen armies for a few seconds. Breaking more of them, especially the generals and their sergeants, gives more loot and Timeless splinters.

  • Splinters combine into Emblems; Emblems open the Domain of Timeless Conflict, which drops Timeless Jewels.
  • Timeless Jewels with the right seed can be very valuable. Check them on trade before selling cheap.
PoEDB: Legionpoe.ninja fragments

Delirium

MediumFast clearSome investment

A Delirium mirror fills the map with fog that makes monsters stronger and adds reward bars. Keep moving forward; the fog fades if you stop. Delirium orbs make maps deliriously difficult from the start.

  • Drops Simulacrum splinters (combine to open the Simulacrum), cluster jewels and delirium orbs.
  • Harder than it looks: deep fog hits hard. Make sure your defences are capped before investing.
PoEDB: Deliriumpoe.ninja delirium orbs

Blight

MediumTower defence

Blight is tower defence: build towers along lanes to stop monsters from reaching the pump. It drops oils, used to anoint amulets and to upgrade blighted maps.

  • Blighted and Blight-ravaged maps give many encounters at once and are the main way to farm it.
  • The rare high-tier oils are where the money is; the common ones are worth little.
PoEDB: Blightpoe.ninja oils

Expedition

EasyAny build

Place explosives over buried remnants and chests, then detonate. Monster modifiers make it harder; reward modifiers make it better, so skip the dangerous ones for your build.

  • Drops artifacts you trade to the Expedition NPCs for gear and currency, or sell in bulk.
  • Logbooks hold a full Expedition area and are good value when the league is fresh.
PoEDB: Expeditionpoe.ninja artifacts

Delve

MediumSulphite needed

The Azurite Mine is an endless dungeon fuelled by sulphite you collect in maps. Deeper is harder but richer.

  • Drops fossils and resonators used for crafting. Some fossils are worth a lot.
  • Fossil nodes off the main path are where most of the value is; use flares to explore the dark.
PoEDB: Delvepoe.ninja fossils

Ritual

Medium

Complete the ritual circles in a map to earn Tribute, then spend it at the last altar. You can defer items to see them again later at a higher price.

  • Omens and other high-value rewards show up in the Ritual shop. Deferring lots of cheap items costs you nothing but Tribute.
PoEDB: Ritualpoe.ninja omens

Harvest

Medium

Grow plots in the Sacred Grove and kill the monsters that hatch. They drop lifeforce, which powers Harvest crafts. Lifeforce itself sells in bulk to crafters.

PoEDB: Harvest

Bossing

HardStrong single target

Pinnacle bosses need fragments to fight and drop rare uniques. You can farm the fragments yourself, buy them, or sell them to people who boss.

  • Selling fragments is often safer profit than running the fight.
  • Uber versions of bosses drop the most expensive uniques in the game but need a very strong build.
  • Many players sell carries, and buy them to get their Voidstones early. Wealthy Exile lists these services.

EasyTarget farming

Every map area drops certain divination cards. Farming one map that drops a valuable card is a classic strategy; PoEDB lists which areas drop which cards.

Selling your loot

  • Currency exchange. For currency and many stackables, trade in-game with no whispers.
  • Bulk trade. Sell big stacks of essences, oils or splinters at a small discount for quick sales. Wealthy Exile has a bulk trade board.
  • Price check first. Use the Market page to price everything you drop.
  • Loot filter. A good filter hides junk so you only pick up what sells. FilterBlade is the usual choice.
